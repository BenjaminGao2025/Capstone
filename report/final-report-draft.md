## Abstract (≤250 words) — owner: Yuze

TODO — draft skeleton:
1. Context: ranking-based schedulers (vLLM-LTR) cut mean TTFT up to 8.1× in-distribution.
2. Problem: they rely on an offline-trained length predictor; robustness under distribution shift is uncharacterized.
3. Discovery: on OOD traffic, ranking quality collapses (tau −0.642 → −0.420), p99 inverts (1.53× worse than FCFS), and at rate 8 preemption churn exhausts swap and crashes the engine.
4. Mitigation: waiting-time aging gate + preemption protection; validated on RTX 3090 — 500/500 completion at rate 8, and Pareto-better than FCFS (mean and p99) at rate 4.
5. Takeaway: deployable gain is ~1.2–1.3×, not the headline 8×; robustness must be co-designed with ranking.
